Steps to prepare a signature scan

  1. Sign on the paper and scan or photograph it clearly, following the application's instructions for ink, paper, and background.
  2. Read the official notice for the accepted file format, pixel or physical dimensions, and minimum or maximum file size.
  3. Open the resizer, set those values, choose a background only if the notice calls for one, and select the scan.
  4. Review the preview for legibility and margins. Download the JPEG and check its properties before uploading it to the application.

Use the values from your own application

Signature specifications are not universal. A portal may impose different dimensions, file formats, size limits, or background rules; a value suitable for one form may be rejected by another. Do not assume the default photo dimensions in the tool are suitable for a signature. Set the signature's exact required dimensions and file-size limit in the fine-tune controls.

For Indian government recruitment, consult the current notice published by the issuing authority, such as the Staff Selection Commission or Union Public Service Commission. Use the actual authority's site for your application; these examples are not exhaustive.

Not an official service: SarkariTools is independent and does not publish or validate government application requirements. The official notice and portal are authoritative.

Limitations and privacy

The tool converts and compresses images in your browser and offers basic brightness, contrast, background, and sharpening controls. It does not certify a signature, recognize handwriting, or guarantee a portal will accept the result. JPEG compression may reduce fine strokes, so inspect the downloaded file at full size before submitting.

Image files are limited to 20 MB and 16 megapixels. The tool does not upload selected images to its own server; your browser and hosting provider still handle the webpage itself.
