How to create the PDF

  1. Check whether the receiving portal accepts PDF and note its page-size, page-count, orientation, and maximum-file-size rules.
  2. Open the tool and select up to 20 image files. Files can be reordered by selecting them in the order you want them to appear.
  3. Choose either A4 pages or fit-to-image pages, enter a filename, then generate and download the PDF.
  4. Open the downloaded PDF and confirm page order, readability, orientation, and file size before submitting it.

PDF options and limits

A4 mode places each image on an A4 page while preserving its aspect ratio. Fit-to-image mode creates a page for each image's dimensions. Images are embedded as JPEGs, so output may not preserve transparency or original image quality exactly.

  • Up to 20 images, with each source file no larger than 20 MB and a combined source size no larger than 80 MB.
  • Supported image pixels are capped at 24 megapixels total across the selected pages.
  • PDF acceptance, page count, and maximum PDF size depend on the application portal. The tool cannot promise a generated PDF will meet an unspecified portal limit.

Check the official submission instructions

Do not assume every application accepts the same PDF settings. Read the current instructions on the receiving authority's official portal. For Indian government recruitment, examples of official sites include the Staff Selection Commission and the Union Public Service Commission; check the issuing body relevant to your application.

Not an official service: SarkariTools is not affiliated with a government body and does not maintain official application requirements. Verify the final PDF on the portal before submitting it.
